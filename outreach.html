Outreach

Philosophiederphysik.de

Together with Kian Salimkhani, I co-founded philosophiederphysik.de in 2018 to make philosophy of physics better known in German-speaking countries. We organise yearly weekend seminars (since 2018) and quarterly lectures on philosophy of science topics (since 2022), and provide career background information and study materials.

EmerGe Collaboration

Within the EmerGe collaboration, six physics colleagues and I work to foster an environment — through retreats, specialised workshops, and ongoing reading sessions — that collectively helps advance our understanding of emergent geometries and spacetimes.
